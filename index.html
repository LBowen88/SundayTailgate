About the League

10 teams. 17 weeks.
One champion.

Sunday Tailgates is a 10-team full PPR league with a 1-keeper format, $100 FAAB, 8 playoff spots and 1 horrific toilet bowl.

🏈

Full PPR Scoring

1 point per reception, 0.5 per rush attempt, 6-point reception or rushing TDs, 4-point passing TDs, -2 for INTs and fumbles lost.

Full Points System Breakdown ↗
🔑

1-Keeper Format

Each manager carries one keeper into the subsequent season's draft, adding a dynasty-lite layer of strategy to every roster decision.

💸

$100 FAAB Waivers

Blind-bid waiver system with a $100 budget. Every add is a strategic spend — rare free pickups, rare free lunches.

🏆

8-Team Playoffs

The top 8 of 10 teams qualify for postseason starting Week 15. Playoffs run through the Week 17 championship.

🚽

The Infamous Toilet Bowl

Where the two found at the pit fight it to the death to escape the league's punishment.
